## First maker comment

Hi Product Hunt — I built Perq because premium credit cards promise a long list of benefits, but actually using them means jumping between issuer pages, PDFs, reward portals and expiry dates.

Perq gives that mess one opinionated interface:

- choose the cards in your wallet;
- see active offers, card benefits and memberships in one place;
- rank them by value, urgency and usefulness;
- search, filter and save what you may use;
- open the official source behind every item.

The public launch is a live India demo covering Times Black, Amex Platinum, HDFC Infinia and Emirates Emeralde. It needs no account and collects no card numbers. If you want your own cards or country, Perq is open source and designed to become a private copy whose generated offers and refresh history stay yours.

I would especially value feedback on one question: does the ranking help you decide which benefit to use, or would you prefer a different default order?
